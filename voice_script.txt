VOICE PROFILE RECORDING GUIDE
=============================

THE NATURAL CONVERSATION METHOD
-------------------------------
The absolute best way to create a voice profile is to record yourself in natural conversation.
Your voice changes dramatically when reading vs. speaking naturally - and the synthesis
model will capture whatever patterns you give it.

RECOMMENDED APPROACH: Phone Call Recording
------------------------------------------
1. Call a friend or family member (use headphones so only your voice is recorded)
2. Aim for 10-15 minutes of conversation (to get 5+ minutes of your speaking)
3. Let the conversation flow naturally - this captures your real voice

CONVERSATION TOPICS TO COVER:
• Catch up on recent events in your lives
• Share a funny story or memory
• Explain something you're passionate about
• Ask their advice on something
• Discuss weekend or vacation plans
• Talk about a movie, book, or show you both know
• Share what you've been learning or working on

ALTERNATIVE SOLO RECORDING OPTIONS:
-----------------------------------
If a phone call isn't practical, try these natural speaking exercises:

1. **Explain a Process** (2-3 minutes)
   - Pick something you know well (cooking a dish, using software, a hobby)
   - Explain it as if teaching a friend who asked for help
   - Include "so first you...", "then make sure to...", "the tricky part is..."

2. **Tell Stories** (2-3 minutes)
   - A funny thing that happened recently
   - A travel experience
   - A childhood memory
   - Meeting someone interesting

3. **Voice Messages** (2-3 minutes)
   - Pretend you're leaving a long voicemail for a friend
   - Update them on your life
   - Ask about their life
   - Make plans together

4. **Opinion Pieces** (1-2 minutes)
   - Your thoughts on a recent movie or book
   - Why you love/hate something specific
   - Your take on a current event (non-controversial)
   - Restaurant or product recommendations

5. **Daily Narration** (1-2 minutes)
   - Describe your morning routine as you do it
   - Narrate making your favorite meal
   - Walk through your workspace explaining things

TECHNICAL RECORDING TIPS:
------------------------
• Use headphones for phone calls (prevents recording the other person)
• Position mic 6-8 inches from mouth
• Find a quiet room with soft furnishings (reduces echo)
• Record in your normal speaking position (sitting/standing as usual)
• Don't worry about "um"s and "uh"s - they're part of natural speech

WHAT TO AVOID:
-------------
• Reading from a script
• Using a "presentation voice"
• Speaking unusually clearly or slowly
• Trying to sound "professional"
• Forcing emotions or expressions

QUALITY CHECK:
-------------
After recording, test with English translation. Ask yourself:
• Does this sound like me in normal conversation?
• Would my friends recognize this as my natural voice?
• Are there varied emotions and tones?

Remember: The goal is to capture how you ACTUALLY speak, not how you think
you should sound. Natural is always better than perfect.

QUICK CHECKLIST:
---------------
□ 5+ minutes of your natural speech
□ Varied emotions and situations
□ Normal speaking pace and style
□ Clear recording without echo
□ Tested with English to verify quality

The more natural and relaxed you are, the better your results will be!
